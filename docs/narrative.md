# The Chuggernaught: Last Orders
## Narrative Design — Version 1.0

**Project:** Roblox Halloween 2026  
**Genre:** Spooky survival adventure / horror-comedy  
**Audience:** 13–14-year-olds  
**Initial experience:** Single-player  
**Future ambition:** 2–4 player cooperative mode  
**Target playtime:** 8–12 minutes  
**Status:** Core narrative approved; gameplay details in development

---

# 1. Narrative Vision

**DRINK. DESTROY. REPEAT.**

*It's Halloween night. Your car has broken down on a deserted country road. With nowhere else to go, you seek shelter in an old pub. But its landlord has been waiting thirty years for his next customer.*

**The Chuggernaught: Last Orders** is a short, atmospheric Halloween survival adventure set inside a mysterious, apparently abandoned English country pub.

The player initially seeks somewhere to stay for the night, but gradually uncovers the legend of a monstrous former landlord known as The Chuggernaught.

What begins as a search for shelter becomes a desperate attempt to escape.

The experience combines exploration, environmental storytelling, suspense, brief monster encounters and a chaotic final chase.

The narrative culminates in the player stealing The Chuggernaught's prized tankard and escaping into the storm.

## Narrative principles

- **Spooky, not terrifying.** Tension comes from darkness, rain, strange noises, anticipation and unexpected encounters.
- **Humour amid the horror.** The Chuggernaught is intimidating but exaggerated, theatrical and occasionally ridiculous.
- **Discovery over exposition.** Players uncover the legend through their surroundings rather than lengthy cutscenes.
- **A memorable antagonist.** The Chuggernaught's personality and behaviour define the experience.
- **A strong finale.** Stealing the tankard transforms a straightforward escape into a memorable confrontation.
- **Keep the mystery.** The precise origins of the monster and his supernatural powers remain unexplained.

---

# 2. Setting — The Crooked Tankard

The game takes place on Halloween night, 31 October 2026, at approximately 9 PM.

The location is a remote English country pub called **The Crooked Tankard**.

The pub stands beside a narrow country road, surrounded by dark woodland and fields.

Heavy rain falls throughout the evening. Occasional thunder illuminates the countryside, and strong winds cause the pub's old wooden sign to creak.

Despite its neglected exterior, warm orange light shines through the windows.

An old sign outside reads:

**THE CROOKED TANKARD — ROOMS AVAILABLE**

The pub appears to offer shelter from the storm.

Inside, however, something is very wrong.

The building contains an empty bar, old furniture, dusty rooms, a kitchen, a cellar and the former landlord's office.

The pub should feel believable as an old English country inn, with Halloween atmosphere layered over its architecture.

### Visual atmosphere

- Heavy rain and wet, reflective surfaces
- Dark blue and purple exterior lighting
- Warm orange light from the pub windows
- Flickering lamps and an old fireplace
- Dusty furniture and aged wooden interiors
- Occasional thunder and lightning
- Shadows, creaking floorboards and distant footsteps
- Stylised, classic blocky Roblox visuals

The atmosphere should be mysterious and slightly unsettling, without graphic horror.

# 3. Backstory — The Legend of The Chuggernaught

Thirty years ago, The Crooked Tankard was a lively country pub.

Its landlord was an enormous, bald, exceptionally strong man known locally as **The Chuggernaught**.

He was famous for his enormous appetite for drink, his terrible temper and his prized wooden tankard.

Every Halloween, the landlord held a legendary drinking competition.

But one Halloween night, something went terribly wrong.

Witnesses reported strange noises coming from the cellar.

The lights went out.

Windows shattered.

An enormous roar echoed across the village.

By morning, the pub was deserted.

The landlord had disappeared, and The Crooked Tankard never reopened.

Over the following decades, rumours spread.

Some believed the landlord had consumed a cursed brew.

Others believed the pub had always been haunted.

A few claimed that something supernatural lived inside his wooden tankard.

Nobody knew the truth.

And nobody dared enter the pub to find out.

### Narrative rule

The exact cause of The Chuggernaught's transformation must remain ambiguous.

Players can discover clues suggesting different explanations, but the game should never definitively establish one.

---

# 4. The Player

The player is an ordinary traveller whose car breaks down while driving along a remote country road on Halloween night.

They have no prior connection to The Crooked Tankard or its former landlord.

Their initial motivation is simple:

**Find shelter and somewhere to stay until morning.**

They are not ghost hunters, investigators or adventurers.

They have no weapons or supernatural abilities.

Their survival depends on curiosity, observation, quick thinking and avoiding The Chuggernaught.

The player should feel vulnerable but capable.

The character's precise appearance and personal identity are not yet defined.

# 5. Narrative Structure

## Prologue — Broken Down

**Location:** Country road outside The Crooked Tankard

The game opens on a deserted country road during a heavy Halloween storm.

The player's car suddenly splutters and stops.

They attempt to restart the engine.

Nothing happens.

Rain lashes against the windscreen.

Their phone has almost no battery, and there is no mobile signal.

Through the rain, the player notices a faint orange glow in the distance.

An old country pub stands beside the road.

Its sign reads:

**THE CROOKED TANKARD — ROOMS AVAILABLE**

With no other apparent shelter nearby, the player leaves the car and approaches the pub.

The front door opens with a long creak.

The player steps inside.

Behind them, the door slams shut.

### Intended emotion

Curiosity, mild unease and relief at finding shelter.

---

## Act I — No Vacancy

**Location:** Main bar and entrance area

The player enters what appears to be an empty but strangely welcoming pub.

A fire crackles in the fireplace.

An old radio plays quietly.

A half-finished drink sits on the bar.

Nobody is present.

The player calls out:

**"Hello? Anyone here?"**

There is no answer.

The player begins exploring, hoping to find the landlord or arrange a room.

Instead, they discover signs that the pub has been abandoned for decades.

Possible discoveries include:

- An old guestbook with its final entry dated 31 October, thirty years earlier.
- A newspaper clipping about the disappearance of the landlord.
- A faded photograph of an enormous bald man holding a wooden tankard.
- Damaged furniture and unexplained marks on the walls.
- A warning scratched into a wooden table.

The warning reads:

**"NEVER LET HIM FINISH HIS DRINK."**

From somewhere beneath the floorboards, the player hears:

*THUD. THUD. GLUG.*

Something is moving below the pub.

The player attempts to leave but discovers the entrance is jammed shut.

An alternative emergency exit exists, but it cannot currently be opened.

### Intended emotion

Mystery and growing suspense.

### Narrative outcome

The player realises that the pub is not what it first appeared to be and begins searching for another way out.

---

## Act II — The Legend

**Location:** Main bar, kitchen, corridors and other accessible rooms

While investigating the pub, the player gradually uncovers the story of its former landlord.

Old photographs, newspaper clippings and handwritten notes reveal the existence of The Chuggernaught.

One note from a former employee reads:

*"He never puts that tankard down. Every time he drinks, he grows stronger. If you ever hear him chugging, run."*

The player learns that The Chuggernaught disappeared thirty years ago during a Halloween drinking competition.

Several clues suggest something supernatural happened in the cellar.

The precise cause remains unknown.

The player also discovers that the emergency exit requires power to be restored.

The old electrical equipment is located beneath the pub.

To escape, they must enter the cellar.

### Intended emotion

Discovery, apprehension and anticipation.

### Narrative outcome

The player understands the legend but has not yet seen the monster.

---

## Act III — The Awakening

**Location:** The cellar

The player descends into the cellar.

The atmosphere becomes noticeably more threatening.

Large wooden barrels line the walls.

Water drips from the ceiling.

The lighting is poor, and strange noises echo through the room.

The player locates the electrical equipment and restores power.

The lights flicker.

For a moment, everything becomes silent.

Then an enormous shadow appears behind the barrels.

A wooden tankard slowly rises into view.

*GLUG. GLUG. GLUG.*

The creature lowers the tankard.

For the first time, the player sees The Chuggernaught.

He is enormous, bald, broad-shouldered and dressed in battered clothing.

He stares at the player.

Then he bellows:

**"LAST ORDERS!"**

The Chuggernaught begins pursuing the player.

The player must escape the cellar and return to the main pub.

### Intended emotion

Surprise, excitement and a short burst of panic.

### Narrative outcome

The monster is revealed and becomes an active threat.

---

## Act IV — The Hunt

**Location:** Main bar, corridors, upstairs rooms and landlord's office

The Chuggernaught now roams the pub.

The player must find the key to the emergency exit.

Clues lead towards the former landlord's office.

While searching, the player must avoid the monster.

The Chuggernaught periodically drinks from his tankard.

Each time he drinks, his strength and aggression increase.

His footsteps grow heavier.

His movements become more unpredictable.

He occasionally roars, stumbles, smashes objects or angrily searches for the player.

Despite his threatening appearance, his exaggerated behaviour creates occasional moments of comedy.

As the player explores, they discover an important secret.

**The Chuggernaught's supernatural strength is connected to his tankard.**

Without it, he becomes temporarily weaker.

The player realises that stealing the tankard may provide the opportunity needed to escape.

### Intended emotion

Sustained suspense interrupted by frantic and humorous encounters.

### Narrative outcome

The player discovers the emergency exit key and learns that the tankard is central to defeating or evading the monster.

---

## Act V — Last Orders

**Location:** Main pub and emergency exit

The player has located the emergency exit key.

However, The Chuggernaught is preventing them from escaping.

The player must create a distraction and wait for an opportunity to take his tankard.

The monster briefly puts the tankard down.

The player grabs it.

Everything becomes silent.

The Chuggernaught looks at his empty hand.

He slowly turns towards the player.

His expression changes from confusion to absolute fury.

**"OI! THAT'S MY TANKARD!"**

He lets out an enormous roar.

The final chase begins.

The player races through the pub carrying the stolen tankard.

The Chuggernaught charges after them, smashing through obstacles and shouting angrily.

The player reaches the emergency exit, unlocks it and escapes into the storm.

### Intended emotion

Adrenaline, comedy and triumph.

### Narrative outcome

The player escapes the pub with the tankard.

---

# 6. Epilogue — One for the Road

**Location:** Outside the pub and inside the player's car

The player bursts through the emergency exit.

Heavy rain pours down.

Behind them, The Chuggernaught crashes against the pub door.

He roars furiously.

The player runs towards their abandoned car, carrying the enormous tankard.

As they approach, the headlights unexpectedly flicker on.

The engine starts.

The player jumps into the car and drives away.

The tankard rests on the passenger seat.

For the first time all evening, everything is quiet.

Then the radio crackles.

A familiar deep voice whispers:

**"DRINK. DESTROY. REPEAT."**

The tankard begins to glow.

The screen cuts to black.

**YOU SURVIVED LAST ORDERS!**

*But did you really escape?*

### Narrative purpose

The ending provides a satisfying escape while leaving the mystery unresolved.

It also creates an opportunity for a future sequel involving the stolen tankard.

# 7. The Chuggernaught — Character Profile

## Identity

**Name:** The Chuggernaught  
**Role:** Main antagonist  
**Type:** NPC monster  
**Former occupation:** Landlord of The Crooked Tankard

## Appearance

The existing Chuggernaught concept artwork is the canonical visual reference.

Key characteristics:

- Very tall and imposing
- Completely bald head
- Exaggerated, muscular, blocky proportions
- Heavy eyebrows and a menacing grin
- Dirty white vest
- Sleeveless black jacket
- Torn dark trousers
- Heavy black boots
- Leather wristbands
- Oversized wooden tankard

The character must remain recognisable as the original concept.

## Personality

The Chuggernaught is:

- Aggressive and territorial
- Loud and theatrical
- Easily angered
- Unpredictable
- Occasionally clumsy
- Slightly ridiculous despite his intimidating size

He should be frightening when pursuing the player but entertaining to watch.

## Signature dialogue

**Primary catchphrase:**

"DRINK. DESTROY. REPEAT."

**First reveal:**

"LAST ORDERS!"

**Tankard theft:**

"OI! THAT'S MY TANKARD!"

**Additional dialogue:** To be developed.

## Signature behaviour

The Chuggernaught periodically drinks from his oversized tankard.

As he drinks, he becomes stronger and more dangerous.

This progression is an important part of both the story and the player's experience.

His signature animation concepts are:

1. Idle
2. Chugging
3. Bigger / stronger
4. Stumbling
5. Attacking
6. Celebrating

The tone should remain cartoonish rather than graphic or realistic.

# 8. The Tankard — Central Narrative Object

The wooden tankard is the most important object in the story after The Chuggernaught himself.

It serves four narrative functions.

**1. Establishes the legend**

The player first sees it in old photographs and historical clues.

**2. Explains the monster's behaviour**

The Chuggernaught repeatedly drinks from it, becoming progressively stronger.

**3. Creates the final objective**

The player discovers that removing the tankard weakens the monster and creates an opportunity to escape.

**4. Provides the final twist**

The tankard begins glowing after the player escapes, suggesting that its supernatural power remains active.

### Visual identity

- Oversized wooden drinking vessel
- Dark wooden body
- Heavy metal bands
- Large handle
- Distinctive silhouette
- Consistent with the original character concept artwork

The tankard should be immediately recognisable throughout the game.

---

# 9. Environmental Storytelling

The story should be communicated primarily through the environment.

Potential storytelling objects include:

| Object | Narrative purpose |
|---|---|
| Old guestbook | Establishes when the pub was last occupied |
| Newspaper clipping | Introduces the landlord's disappearance |
| Landlord photograph | Reveals The Chuggernaught's former identity |
| Employee's handwritten note | Explains his obsession with the tankard |
| Damaged furniture | Suggests his extraordinary strength |
| Cellar barrels | Connect the pub's history to the monster's awakening |
| Landlord's office | Reveals the importance of the emergency exit |
| Warning about the tankard | Foreshadows the final objective |

The player should be able to understand the basic story without finding every optional clue.

---

# 10. Tone and Content Guidelines

The game is intended for 13–14-year-old players.

The target tone is:

- 60% spooky atmosphere and suspense
- 30% humour and chaos
- 10% sudden scares

These percentages are creative guidelines, not gameplay requirements.

### Encouraged

- Creepy sound effects
- Thunder and lightning
- Strange shadows
- Exaggerated monster reactions
- Short, surprising encounters
- Comical dialogue
- Cartoon destruction
- Dramatic chases

### Avoid

- Graphic violence or gore
- Disturbing imagery
- Excessively intense jump scares
- Realistic injury
- Prolonged helplessness
- Alcohol consumption by the player
- Rewarding or encouraging real-world drinking

The Chuggernaught's drinking is part of his fictional monster identity, not a behaviour the player is encouraged to imitate.

---

# 11. Narrative Design Constraints

The initial game is single-player.

The narrative should remain adaptable to a future cooperative experience for 2–4 players.

Future multiplayer support should not require rewriting the monster's backstory or the central escape objective.

The initial experience should target approximately 8–12 minutes, although the final duration will depend on playtesting.

The story should be understandable with minimal dialogue and limited cutscenes.

The focus is on playable storytelling rather than lengthy cinematic sequences.

- The player's car breaks down on a deserted country road.

- The pub appears deserted despite showing signs of recent activity.
- The player gradually uncovers the legend of The Chuggernaught.

# 13. Open Narrative and Gameplay Questions

The following details are not yet finalised and should not be treated as approved requirements.

1. **Car breakdown:** Is it a coincidence, or did the pub somehow cause the car to fail?
2. **Pub entrance:** Does the front door slam shut supernaturally, or is there a more grounded explanation?
3. **The legend:** Which clues are mandatory, and which are optional discoveries?
4. **The tankard:** Why does it weaken The Chuggernaught when removed, and how quickly does that happen?
5. **The theft:** What exact distraction allows the player to take the tankard?
6. **Getting caught:** Does the player receive a second chance, get imprisoned temporarily or lose immediately?
7. **Escape:** How does the player reach the car from the emergency exit?
8. **Car restarting:** Is it supernatural, coincidental or connected to the tankard?
9. **Dialogue:** What additional catchphrases and reactions should The Chuggernaught have?
10. **Replayability:** Should clue locations, item positions or encounters vary between playthroughs?

These questions should be addressed during gameplay and level design.

# 15. Creative North Star

The game should deliver one memorable experience:

*A stranded traveller enters a mysterious country pub on a rainy Halloween night, discovers the legend of its monstrous landlord, survives a series of increasingly chaotic encounters, steals his supernatural tankard and escapes into the storm.*

The Chuggernaught should be a character players remember, laugh about and want to encounter again.

**DRINK. DESTROY. REPEAT.**
