# Halloween deadline scope

Canonical narrative: [narrative.md](narrative.md), preserved as found.

Target: one short single-player escape through The Firefly Tavern. Implement a greybox route, a small objective chain, one monster encounter, a room search for the exit key and a clear escape ending. Tankard theft is no longer required under the approved 27 October revision. Use placeholders until approved assets arrive. The narrative targets 8–12 minutes; validate through playtesting.

Defer cooperative play, monetisation, general progression saving, additional AI systems and extra levels. Open gameplay questions in the narrative remain unresolved; this scaffold does not decide them.

The local release candidate includes the eight-room pub/cellar puzzle, random exit key, server-owned hunt, bell distraction, two-hit survival, courtyard/car escape, storm, original prop dressing and gothic presentation. Mobile objectives are compact with a red two-half health dial at the top left. First-view scenes are mandatory; completed scenes support hold-to-skip and only scene-seen flags persist per account. The current first interaction is a staff note explaining the locked exit/power/key, rather than a drink-prevention objective.

Remaining release gates are published single-player capacity, cross-visit scene-history verification, real-device/father-son acceptance and explicit publication approval. See [RELEASE.md](RELEASE.md) and [PLAYTEST.md](PLAYTEST.md).

Current playtest priorities and release follow-ups are tracked in [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md).
