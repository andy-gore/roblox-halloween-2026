Prepared 29 October 2026. Local review package only; nothing has been published or uploaded.

## Listing

**Name:** The Chuggernaught: Last Orders

**Short description:** A Halloween dare. An abandoned tavern. One very angry landlord. Restore the lights, find the key and escape.

**Description:**

It's Halloween night, and you've accepted a dare: get the lights working inside the abandoned Firefly Tavern.

Then the entrance collapses behind you.

Explore the cellar, restore power and search the guest rooms and office for the emergency exit key. Stay ahead of the Chuggernaught, use the service bell to draw him away, and race through the storm to your car.

• A short single-player spooky adventure.
• Two health points: survive the first catch and use the recovery window to get away.
• The exit key can appear in a different room each run.
• First-view story scenes play in full; returning players can hold to skip completed scenes.
• Keyboard, touch and controller support. Follow the on-screen interaction prompts.

Early test build. Tell us what felt unclear, unfair or especially spooky.

## Player instructions

Move with the normal Roblox controls. On a phone, use the movement stick, drag to look, and tap the interaction prompts. On keyboard, use movement keys and the displayed interaction key. Controller users should follow the displayed button prompts.

The red health circle and notebook objective icon sit together at the top left on phones; tap the notebook for instructions. Desktop uses a short objective strip with a plus button. The staff note opens as a reading card with a Close button. The cellar switches have no reading overlay. The health circle has two separated halves; an outlined half is lost health. After the first catch, move away while the monster recovers. Ringing the bell always makes a sound; its distraction effect has a cooldown.

Read the staff note, find the cellar panel, work out the circuit order from the switches and indicator lights, restore the lights, search for the key, unlock the emergency exit and reach the car.
